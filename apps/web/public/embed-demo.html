Dealer embed: demo

Two made-up dealer websites, each with our configurator in its own look, at its own prices (our B2B price plus its margin; our B2B price never reaches this page). The first sends the order to the dealer, the second pays in our checkout in its look. Nothing is ordered here: these demo dealers never make an order. Below them: the frame for site builders that refuse scripts, and the events a dealer's page receives.
