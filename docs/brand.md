# S2DIO Industries: house style (for the website)

Source: the brand book by IS Creative (Client Area on Notion, received 5 October 2026). The
logos are in `apps/web/public/brand/s2dio/` (SVG; `website-*` for the site header, `only-*`
for the mark alone, `main-*` and `smaller-*` for the full logo, `type-*` for the word mark),
each in colour, black and white.

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Pantone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S2dio Off White | `#F1F2F2` | 241 242 242 | 0 0 0 5 | P 179-1 |
| S2dio Light Green | `#778074` | 119 128 116 | 52 37 50 15 | P 177-7 |
| S2dio Green | `#6E776B` | 110 119 107 | 52 37 50 23 | P 177-9 |
| S2dio Dark Green | `#3C443C` | 60 68 60 | 52 37 50 65 | P 177-16 |

- **Type:** Work Sans (primary), DM Sans (substitute), FontAwesome (icons). Work Sans and DM Sans
  are on Google Fonts.
- **Look** (from the applications): calm and natural; off-white paper; photography of outdoor
  furniture and woven materials, cut out in arches with rounded corners, echoing the mark; a lot
  of space; the logo small and bottom right.
