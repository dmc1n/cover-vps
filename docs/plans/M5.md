# M5 plan: finished patterns for the cutting table

Written 2026-09-30 while the owner was offline (owner: "continue with M5, M6, M7"); the owner
reviews it afterwards. Values that are not confirmed are defaults marked "to confirm" in
`config/defaults.yaml` and listed in `docs/QUESTIONS.md`.

## Context

M4 gives flat panels, seam to seam, without allowances (`pattern.json`, `pattern.dxf`). M5 turns
them into what the CNC table cuts: every panel with its seam allowances and hem, the air vents,
and pen marks for sewing, plus a cutting list. The paper test of M4 is skipped (owner); the first
physical test is a cover cut on the CNC table from this export.

## What the owner gets

`cover export models/<id>` (and `cover run` does it too) writes, next to the M4 files:

- `cut.dxf`: every panel as it is cut: outline with allowances on the CUT layer, the vent
  openings on the CUT layer (deliberate openings, rule 7), and on the PEN layer: the stitch line
  (the seam-to-seam outline), the hem fold line, matching marks, panel name and number, "to P3"
  at each seam, the UP arrow, vent hood placement, model name and pattern revision.
- `cut.svg`: the same to view or print at 1:1.
- `cutting-list.pdf`: every piece (panels, vent hoods, vent membranes) with number, quantity,
  size, area, and the fabric needed on a 150 cm roll.
- `finished.json`: the finished PatternSet (FORMATS.md).

## Rules (all parameters; "to confirm" where not yet confirmed)

- **Double-stitched seams** (`construction.method: double_stitch`, today's practice):
  `stitching.allowance_mm` (15) on both panels of every seam (`stitching.sides: both`, to
  confirm; `lap` puts it on the lap-side panel only).
- **Welded seams** (`welded`): `welding.overlap_mm` (30) on the lap-side panel only; a weld
  guide line on the under panel where the upper panel's edge lands (`welding.guide_line`).
- Corners where two allowances meet are closed square (the two allowances extended until they
  meet), so the cut outline is one clean line.
- **Hem:** `hem.allowance_mm` (50) below the hem line, the fold line on PEN. Cord exits
  (`hem.cord_exits`, 2) as pen marks, spread evenly; no cuts (to confirm).
- **Air vents** (owner, 2026-09-30): 25 × 22 cm, bottom edge 5 cm above the lower end of the
  cover, one per full metre of hem, at least one, spread evenly along the hem, kept clear of
  vertical seams (`features.vent_seam_clearance_mm`, 100). The opening is cut in the skirt
  panel; the hood and the membrane are separate pieces (`features.vent_hood_depth_mm` 80,
  allowances as the seams; to confirm). If the skirt is too low for a vent (skirt height below
  5 + 22 cm plus allowance), a warning says so.

## Technical approach

- `finish/allowance.py`: per panel, each edge (seam or hem) offset outward by its own distance
  (shapely single-sided buffers, square caps extended to meet the neighbours), united with the
  panel; the result simplified to 0.1 mm. Welded under panels get distance 0 on that edge.
- `finish/features.py`: vent positions along the hem (arc length over all skirt panels, evenly,
  nudged away from vertical seams), the opening rectangle in panel coordinates (hem edge
  direction and UP), hood and membrane pieces. Cord-exit marks.
- `finish/finish.py`: builds the finished PatternSet from `pattern.json`.
- `export/cut.py`: DXF (SheetWriter, CUT/PEN, M0 conventions) and SVG; `export/cutlist.py`:
  PDF with a shelf-packing estimate of the roll length.
- CLI `cover export`, and `cover run` calls it.

## Tests

- A 100 × 100 mm square with four stitched seams becomes 130 × 130; welded: only the lap-side
  edges grow; hem edge grows by 50 with the fold line on the old edge.
- Vent count for 0.9 m, 1.0 m, 6.3 m of hem (1, 1, 6); vents stay 100 mm from vertical seams;
  a skirt too low gives the warning.
- SVG size equals the sheet; DXF and SVG byte-identical on repeat; every cut outline simple.

## Physical gate

The first cover cut on the CNC table from `cut.dxf`, sewn and fitted. The owner reports; the
M5 report records it. Until then M6 (web app) proceeds in parallel.
