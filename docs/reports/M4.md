# M4 report: flat patterns

Date: 2026-09-30. Status: done, without the paper test (owner: skipped; the first physical
test is a CNC cut, after M5).

## What works now

```
uv run cover flatten models/<id>
```

turns every panel of the cover into an exact flat pattern piece, in true millimetres:

- **Flat pieces that keep their lengths:** each piece is laid out flat so that lengths on the
  cover stay lengths on the fabric (SLIM). The program reports, per piece, how much the fabric
  would have to stretch to take the 3D shape (the limit is 2 %).
- **Seams that match:** both pieces along a seam are the same length; any difference is
  reported as ease, to spread between the matching marks.
- **Pen marks:** piece name, "to …" at each seam, matching marks every 30 cm, and an UP arrow.
- **Smooth lines (owner rule, all covers):** every edge of every piece is checked for zig-zag.
  A line that strays more than 2 mm is reported.
- **The skirt at one height all round (owner rule, all covers):** straight strips. Where the
  furniture's top edge is clearly higher (the back of a chair), the upright part is a separate
  wall piece, so the top does not have to wrap over its edge.
- **Size drawing (`sizes.pdf`):** the cover drawn like your workshop drawings (top view, front
  and side view, 3D view, all sizes seam to seam, title block), the sizes next to your cover's
  where there is one, and one page per flat piece with the length of every edge.
- **Files:** `pattern.dxf` (all pieces 1:1, cut and pen layers), `pattern.svg`,
  `pattern-stretch.svg` (the stretch per piece in colour), `pattern.json`, `sizes.pdf`.
- **One command for everything:** `cover run <3D file>`; `cover diff old.json new.json` shows
  what changed after a change of settings.

How to use it: `docs/handbook/patterns.md`.

## The Blocchi

| piece | flat size | stretch |
|---|---|---|
| skirt-front, skirt-back, skirt-right | 23.6 cm high, straight strips | 0.3 % |
| wall-front, wall-back, wall-right | up to 18, 8 and 6 cm high | 1.7–2.6 % |
| top-1 (main), top-2 (left end) | 114 × 198, 79 × 133 cm | 4.9 %, 7.5 % |
| top-3 (band), top-4, top-5 (back) | | 2.6 %, 3.1 %, 3.6 % |

- The skirt pieces are right: straight, at one height.
- The top pieces stretch more than the fabric allows, most at the rounded left end. On this 3D
  model the ends of the cushions are rounded in two directions, and one piece cannot lie flat
  there. Extra seams would help, but first we need to know whether the 3D model is right: its
  sizes differ a lot from your cover (question 1 in `docs/QUESTIONS.md`).
- The cover surface of this model has small steps along its edge where the top slopes gently
  (the surface is built on a 5 mm grid), and the wall seams follow them. The zig-zag check
  reports these (6–7 mm). With a smoother surface (`hull.smoothing: 1`) the steps go, but then
  the program leaves the wall pieces out. To be improved with the right model.

## Decisions (DECISIONS.md)

- ADR-027: flattening with SLIM, stretch measured on 99.5 % of each piece's area.
- ADR-028: the size drawing (`sizes.pdf`).
- ADR-029: the level skirt, wall pieces, and the zig-zag check.
