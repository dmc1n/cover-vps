# M2 report: the cover surface (drape hull)

Date: 2026-09-30. Status: done. Tested on the test furniture and on the Blocchi sofa.

## What works now

One command works out the shape a tight fabric cover takes over an imported model:

```
uv run cover hull models/<id>
```

- The cover hangs straight down from the widest point. Legs, undercuts and the space under a
  table disappear behind a vertical skirt.
- Gaps narrower than the bridge gap (default 60 mm: slats, the gaps between cushions) are
  spanned. Wider recesses (seat to backrest) are followed.
- Everywhere the fabric keeps the clearance (default 10 mm) from the furniture. This is checked
  exactly against the model, not on a grid, and repaired where needed. Top edges are rounded
  with the clearance as radius.
- The cover ends at the hem height (default 50 mm above the floor) with a clean, straight edge.
- Output next to the model:
  - `hull.glb`: the cover surface, used by the next steps.
  - `preview.glb`: the furniture in grey and the cover in see-through blue. Drag it into a glTF
    viewer to look at it.
  - `hull.json`: the check (closest distance, fabric area, size, hem length, sharp ridges) and
    every setting used.
- Settings per trial run (`--clearance 15`), per model (`cover.json`) or for everything
  (`config/defaults.yaml`). Boxes in `cover.json` can leave things out (a pole) or fill them in
  (a gap).

How to use it: `docs/handbook/cover-surface.md`.

## Decisions made on the way (details in DECISIONS.md, ADR-024)

- **Faster and more exact than planned.** Because the fabric hangs straight down, the cover can
  be computed from a "height map" (the highest point above each spot on the floor) instead of a
  full 3D grid. That made it both exact at the skirts and fast enough for large models.
- **Fitted covers (not hanging straight down) are not built yet.** The setting
  `hull.sweep_down: false` exists but says "not available yet". It is meant for cushion-like
  items; tell me when a model needs it.
- **"Hard edges the cover must follow"** moves to M3 (seams), where a seam can hold such an
  edge.
- **The bridge gap sags a little.** A gap somewhat narrower than the setting is not quite flat:
  a 20 mm gap sags about 1 mm at the default 60 mm. That is well within the ±5 mm tolerance.
- Five new internal settings (smoothing and remeshing passes, clearance repair passes, grid
  size limit), all in `config/defaults.yaml`.
