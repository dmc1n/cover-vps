# M2 report: the cover surface

Date: 2026-09-30. Status: done, revised the same day after the owner's reference cover and the
rule that water must always run off.

## What works now

One command works out the shape of the cover for an imported model:

```
uv run cover hull models/<id>
```

- **Pulled tight on top.** The top runs in straight lines between the furniture's highest parts:
  from the front edge of the seat straight up to the top of the back, as on your Blocchi cover.
  It never sags into a seat or a recess.
- **Straight down at the sides**, from the widest point to the hem, hiding legs and undercuts.
- **Clearance everywhere:** at least 10 mm from the furniture (default), checked exactly against
  the model and repaired where needed.
- **Open at the bottom**, with a straight hem at the hem height (default 50 mm).
- **Run-off check:** every run reports whether water runs off, or where it would stay (hollows
  and flat patches).
- **Balloon for flat tops:** `--set hull.support=balloon` puts a dome under the middle of a
  flat top so the cover forms a tent. The program finds the lowest balloon height that sheds
  water (69 mm for the 1.6 m test table).
- Output next to the model: `hull.glb` (the cover), `preview.glb` (furniture grey, cover
  see-through blue, for any glTF viewer) and `hull.json` (the check and every setting used).

How to use it: `docs/handbook/cover-surface.md`.

## Decisions made on the way (DECISIONS.md, ADR-024 and ADR-025)

- **Water must run off** is now a rule for every cover (CLAUDE.md rule 12).
- **The tight top is the furniture's convex hull**, clipped to its outline: the shape of a sheet
  pulled infinitely tight. A softer "membrane" was tried first and sagged between the front and
  the back, so it was dropped.
- **Flat means flatter than 5° over at least 10 cm**, away from the edge. Both numbers are
  settings, and 5° is still to be confirmed with you.
- **Balloon:** a dome of 30 cm diameter under the middle of the flat area (to confirm).
- Fitted covers (not hanging straight down) remain "not available yet", and hard edges move to
  M3.

## Questions for you (defaults are used until you answer)

1. **The flat band on top of the back cushions:** on your cover, does water stay there, or do
   the rounded cushions underneath make it run off? If it runs off, I will stop counting a band
   like that as a problem. If water stays, it needs a support too.
2. **The outline at the bottom:** yours is 668 cm, the calculated one 630 cm. Is your cover
   deliberately looser at the bottom (ease to pull it on and off, or room for the elastic)? If
   so, how much ease do you add?
3. **Minimum slope for run-off:** 5° is assumed. Is there a value you work with?
4. **Balloon:** is a dome in the middle of the table what you use? How big is it, and how high
   do you usually set it?
5. **Hem height:** your cover ends at the bottom of the sofa's base (about 6 cm above the floor).
   Should the hem height be "the bottom of the main body" for sofas like this, rather than a
   fixed 5 cm?
