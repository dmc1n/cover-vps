# M6 report: the web app

Date: 2026-09-30. Status: first version running on the server. Still open: the public address
with login through Cloudflare (needs your account, question 14), and the backup to Cloudflare
R2 (same account). Built while you were offline, from the plan in `docs/plans/M6.md`.

## Open it

On your laptop:

```
ssh -L 8080:localhost:8080 dev@168.119.50.82
```

keep that window open, and go to **http://localhost:8080**. The Blocchi is already in it.

## What works now

- **Models:** the list with progress (five dots), panels, worst stretch and warnings. Upload a
  new 3D file with units and up direction; everything runs by itself.
- **A model**, tab by tab:
  - **3D:** furniture, cover surface (water spots red) and panels, each on or off; turn and
    zoom with the mouse.
  - **Seams:** the top view of the cover with contour lines, so you see where it folds. Your
    seams in blue: drag points, draw a new seam across the top (click, click, double-click),
    add or move vertical skirt seams, delete. **Save and recut** runs seams, patterns and cut
    pieces again. **Use automatic seams** forgets the hand-placed ones.
  - **Patterns:** the flat pieces and the stretch picture, with a table.
  - **Size drawing:** `sizes.pdf` in the page.
  - **Cut pieces:** what the machine cuts, the cutting list, and the `cut.dxf` download.
  - **Settings:** all settings with their explanation and where the value comes from.
    **Try without saving** or **Save for this model**. After a run: which settings changed and
    which pieces changed size by more than 1 mm.
  - **Downloads** and **Warnings and log**.
- **Runs** go into a queue, one at a time, with progress per step.
- **Backup:** every night at 02:30 the data goes to `~/backups` (the last 14 kept); a restore
  was tested. To Cloudflare R2 as soon as the account is there.

How to use it: `docs/handbook/web-app.md`.
