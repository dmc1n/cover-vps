# The owner's 115 cover drawings (Covers & all), analysed 1 October 2026

What the owner sent: one zip with 115 technical drawings of earlier covers (PDF, one page each,
views with sizes in cm and inches, a table with fabric, colour, tie downs, grommets). No 3D
models. Read by `scripts/drawings.py`: the texts by the program, each page as a picture by
DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Results: `out/drawings/covers-and-all/drawings.csv` (one row per cover) and
`drawings.json`. Spot checks by eye (D1, S43, C1, T1, R1): shapes, sizes, air pockets and piece
counts right; the furniture type is sometimes wrong (T1 called a sofa), so the groups below
follow the codes in the file names.
