# M3 report: seams and panels

Date: 2026-09-30. Status: done. The automatic top seams on the Blocchi differ from your cover
(see "The Blocchi against your cover"); you can place them yourself in `seams.json`.

## What works now

One command divides the cover surface into panels:

```
uv run cover cut models/<id>
```

- **Skirt seam** all round, where the vertical skirt meets the top.
- **Vertical skirt seams** at every sharp corner, in the middle of the bend, and extra ones
  when a skirt stretch is longer than 3 m. A round skirt without corners gets one seam at the
  back.
- **Roll width:** a top panel too wide for the roll (1480 mm usable) is split. First it tries
  a seam at constant height, which sheds water like roof tiles; if that would give a panel that
  cannot lie flat, a straight seam along the panel's length.
- **Checks on every panel:** it lies flat in one piece, and its flat size fits the roll. Every
  seam knows its two panels, its length, which panel laps over the other (top over skirt, higher
  over lower, front over side) and how tightly it curves.
- **Symmetric furniture gets symmetric panels:** the cover is built for one half and mirrored,
  so the left and right panels are identical.
- **Your own seams:** `seams.json` with points and lines in plan view (from above). The seams
  the program chose are written to `seams.auto.json` in the same format, to copy and edit.
- **Output:** `panels.glb` (each panel in its own colour on the furniture, for the viewer),
  `panels.json` (panels and seams) and `seams.auto.json`.

How to use it: `docs/handbook/seams.md`.

## Decisions (DECISIONS.md, ADR-026)

- Seams are cut exactly into the cover and shared by both panels, so seam lengths always agree.
- Manual seams are given in plan view (from above), not as points on the 3D surface. For these
  covers that is exact, and it is what a plan-view editor in the web app (M6) will produce.
- Tight curves are listed for every cover and warned about for welded covers only.
