# M5 report: finished pieces for the cutting table

Date: 2026-09-30. Status: software done; **the physical test is open**: the first cover cut on
the CNC table and sewn. Built while you were offline, from the plan in `docs/plans/M5.md`; the
rules marked "to confirm" are questions 5 to 10 in `docs/QUESTIONS.md`.

## What works now

```
uv run cover export models/<id>
```

(`cover run` does it too) writes the pieces as they are cut:

- `cut.dxf` for the CNC table: on the CUT layer the cut line of every piece (with seam
  allowances and hem) and the air vent openings; on the PEN layer the stitch line, the hem fold
  line, matching marks, "P5 SKIRT-RIGHT", "TO P2 WALL" at each seam, the UP arrow, where the vent
  hood goes, the cord exits, and the pattern revision.
- `cut.svg` to look at or print, `cutting-list.pdf` with every piece and about how much fabric
  it takes, `finished.json` for the web app.
- **Seam allowances:** stitched seams 15 mm on both pieces; welded seams (per model) 30 mm on the
  piece that laps over, and a pen line on the other piece where the edge must land.
- **Hem:** 50 mm, with the fold line drawn.
- **Air vents:** 25 × 22 cm, 5 cm above the hem, one per full metre of hem, spread evenly and at
  least 10 cm from a vertical seam; the opening is cut out, the hood and the membrane are pieces
  of their own.

How to use it: `docs/handbook/export.md`.
