# Learning from your own covers

Your own drawings and their 3D models, calculated by the program and put beside your drawing,
so we can compare and learn. The upload is on **Covers → New cover**; the results are on
**Covers → Learning**.

## Upload: Covers → New cover, reference pairs

One zip with your drawings and their 3D models (editors and admins):

- **Pairs by name:** `1.step` + `1.pdf`, `2.stp` + `2.pdf`, `kota.glb` + `kota.pdf`. Any 3D
  format the program reads works (STEP, IGES, STL, OBJ, PLY, GLB). Folders inside the zip do not
  matter; a file without a partner is listed and left out.
- **A zip with only the product list** (no drawings) goes to the product list, the same as
  Covers → Product list ([desk.md](desk.md), *The product list*).
- **What happens:** every pair is calculated like any model (a few minutes per model, three at a
  time), and your drawing is kept beside it. The program reads every text and size in the
  drawing.

## Results: Covers → Learning

**Covers → Learning** (`#/covers/learning`, everyone) shows the uploaded batches and their
results only; to upload, it links to New cover.

- **Each pair** with your drawing and the calculated cover side by side.
- **On a cover's page**, **Compare → Your drawing** shows both, with the sizes from your
  drawing next to the calculated ones.

Drawings made on a computer (like the Blocchi drawing) are read exactly. A scanned or
photographed drawing is marked: its lines cannot be read.
